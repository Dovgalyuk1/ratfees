How it works

four rules, none of them written down anywhere

There is no dashboard, no allowlist and nothing to sign. There is a rat and there is your kitchen.

01

It runs

It goes down the chain wallet by wallet on its own, reading who is trading and how carelessly. Busy wallets leave fees lying around like crumbs on a counter, and it can smell a busy wallet from several blocks away.

wallet after wallet · nobody points it anywhere
02

It takes a crumb

One crumb off one fee, in the same block, and then it leaves. It never cleans out a wallet and it never comes back to the same one twice in a night, which is exactly why nobody bothers doing anything about it.

small enough that arguing costs more
03

It gets seen

Wallets do look up, usually at the worst possible moment. Anything the rat is carrying when it gets spotted is dropped on the floor right there, and a dropped crumb is gone for everyone, including him.

caught = the crumb stays on the floor
04

It gets home

Everything that makes it back through the hole is split in the same block: half goes to everyone holding $RAT and the rest burns. Nothing to claim, nothing to stake, no forms — you either hold the thing or you are the kitchen.

half to holders · the rest burns
